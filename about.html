ABOUT FLOW VELLO

We build the systems that make businesses run better.

Flow Vello helps businesses replace repetitive work, disconnected systems and inefficient processes with custom automation, AI agents and software built around the way their teams actually work.

Solve the operational problem first.

We don't automate for the sake of automation.

We first understand how your business works, where time is being lost and which processes are creating unnecessary friction. Then we design and engineer a system that removes those problems.

Our goal isn't to add more technology to your business.
It's to make your existing business work better.

HOW WE WORK

From bottleneck to better system.

A clear six-stage delivery process keeps scope, expectations and technical decisions visible from the first conversation to post-launch support.

01

Discovery: Find the Bottleneck

Understand your current process, systems, team structure, goals and the work that should stop being manual.

02

Strategy: Design the Roadmap

Define requirements, automation opportunities, integrations, data flows and the right technical approach.

03

Proposal: Make the Scope Clear

Receive a transparent plan covering deliverables, milestones, assumptions and the expected implementation path.

04

Build: Execute the System

Build integrations, workflows, agents, dashboards or software with regular progress communication.

05

QA & Launch: Test Before Release

Validate workflows, edge cases, permissions and user journeys before moving the system into production.

06

Support: Improve After Launch

Monitor the system, fix issues and evolve the automation as your business and processes change.
