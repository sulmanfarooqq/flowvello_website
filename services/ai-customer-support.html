COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Practical answers regarding implementation, maintenance, security, and timelines.

How do you guarantee the AI will not hallucinate or make false promises?

We configure strict retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) prompts that instruct the model to answer solely from your approved knowledge base. If an inquiry falls outside the documentation, the AI politely offers to route to human staff.

How easily can we update our business information?

You maintain complete control over the knowledge source (such as a Google Doc, Notion workspace, or FAQ database). When you edit policies, the agent updates automatically.

Which chat widgets or platforms are supported?

We can deploy lightweight chat widgets directly into your website (Webflow, WordPress, custom HTML) or integrate into Zendesk, Freshdesk, and Intercom.

What happens when a customer requests a human?

The agent recognizes handoff keywords or frustrated sentiment and immediately prompts for customer contact details, creates an urgent helpdesk ticket, and notifies your team via Slack.

Is customer conversation data kept private and secure?

Yes. We configure enterprise API endpoints with zero-data-retention agreements so your company documentation and customer chats are never used to train public models.
