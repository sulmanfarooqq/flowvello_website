COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Practical answers regarding implementation, maintenance, security, and timelines.

Can you migrate our existing store from WooCommerce, Magento, or BigCommerce to Shopify?

Yes. We execute complete store migrations including customer accounts, order history, product catalogs, collections, SEO redirects, and payment gateways with zero loss of data or rankings.

What is the difference between standard Shopify and Shopify Plus?

Shopify Plus is built for high-volume enterprise brands ($1M+ annual revenue) offering customizable checkout code, dedicated API rate limits, wholesale B2B portals, and automated workflow scripts. We build on both.

How do you ensure apps do not slow down our storefront?

We avoid unnecessary third-party apps by custom coding features (such as mega menus, size guides, FAQ accordions, and slide-out carts) directly into the Liquid theme.

Can we offer recurring subscriptions and product bundles?

Yes. We integrate industry-standard subscription platforms (such as Recharge or native Shopify Subscriptions) and build custom bundle builders that boost your average order value.

Do you handle post-launch Shopify support and marketing integrations?

Yes. We offer continuous e-commerce maintenance, A/B testing, speed audits, and full integration with Klaviyo email flows and Google Analytics 4.
