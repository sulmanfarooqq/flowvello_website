FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions.

Find answers to common questions about our products and processes.

General

What does Flow Vello actually automate?

We automate repetitive business workflows, customer conversations, lead handling, support tasks, data movement and reporting processes.

Will AI replace our support or sales team?

No. We automate the repetitive layer and keep humans in the loop for exceptions, sensitive cases and decisions that require judgment.

Technology & Integration

Can you work with our existing tools?

Yes. We design integrations around the tools already used by the business where practical, instead of replacing everything unnecessarily. We commonly integrate with Hubspot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Stripe, Shopify, and custom APIs.

Is our data secure when using your AI agents?

Absolutely. We use enterprise-grade APIs where data is never used to train public models. We implement strict access controls and vector databases that remain entirely isolated to your organization.

Process & Support

How long does an automation project take?

Simple workflow projects can be delivered in a few weeks; multi-system AI agents and dashboards require more discovery, testing and integration work and can take 1-3 months. Exact timing is scoped before build.

Do you provide post-launch support?

Yes. Support and optimization continue after launch so the system remains reliable as workflows, tools and business requirements change.

Pricing

How is pricing structured?

We do not charge hourly. Every project is scoped with a fixed, transparent investment based on the complexity of the workflow, integrations required, and the expected business impact.

Are there ongoing fees?

We offer ongoing maintenance and support retainers to ensure your systems stay updated when APIs change or your business processes evolve. You will also be responsible for the direct software costs (e.g., API usage, server hosting, third-party licenses).

What's slowing your business down?

Tell us where your team is losing time, dealing with repetitive work or struggling with disconnected systems. We'll identify where automation, AI or custom software can create the biggest operational improvement.
