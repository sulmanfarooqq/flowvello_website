FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Find answers to common questions about our automation, AI agents, custom software, and how we work.

General

What does Flow Vello actually automate?

We automate repetitive business workflows, customer conversations, lead handling, support tasks, data movement and reporting processes. Every automation is built around your existing tools and team structure — not a generic template.

Can you work with our existing tools?

Yes. We design integrations around the tools already used by the business where practical, instead of replacing everything unnecessarily. We connect CRMs, messaging platforms, databases, ERPs and thousands of other business endpoints.

What industries do you serve?

We work across ecommerce, real estate, SaaS, service businesses, healthcare, logistics, finance, and more. The technology changes by industry — the goal stays the same: fewer manual steps, faster response, and better visibility.

Projects & Process

How long does an automation project take?

Simple workflow projects can be delivered quickly; multi-system AI agents and dashboards require more discovery, testing and integration work. Exact timing is scoped before build so there are no surprises.

How does your process work?

We follow a six-stage process: Discovery, Strategy, Proposal, Build, QA & Launch, and Support. Each stage keeps scope, expectations and technical decisions visible from the first conversation to post-launch.

Do you provide post-launch support?

Yes. Support and optimization can continue after launch so the system remains reliable as workflows, tools and business requirements change.

AI & Automation

Will AI replace our support or sales team?

No. We automate the repetitive layer and keep humans in the loop for exceptions, sensitive cases and decisions that require judgment. AI handles the routine so your team can focus on high-value work.

How secure is the AI you build?

Enterprise-grade security is part of every system. Your proprietary data stays yours — we build with strict access controls, data privacy, and policy-aware response logic baked in from the start.

Can AI agents handle complex multi-step workflows?

Yes. Our custom AI agents are task-focused — they use your business information and execute defined workflows, not just chat. They can qualify leads, route tickets, update CRMs, trigger notifications and escalate when needed.

Pricing

How much does automation cost?

Pricing depends on the scope, complexity, number of integrations and ongoing support requirements. Simple workflow automations cost significantly less than full AI agent systems with dashboards. We scope everything transparently before any commitment.

Do you offer ongoing retainers?

Yes. Many clients prefer a monthly retainer for continuous optimization, monitoring, and new feature development. We can discuss what makes sense after understanding your needs.
