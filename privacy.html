Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information.

Last Updated: January 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when you fill out a contact form, request a strategy call, or communicate with us via email or other channels. This may include your name, email address, phone number, company name, and details about your project.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to provide, maintain, and improve our services, communicate with you about your project, and respond to your inquiries. We do not sell or share your personal information with third-party marketers.

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. When building AI systems for clients, we utilize enterprise-grade APIs and ensure client data is never used to train public language models.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at contact@flowvello.com.
