FAQ

THE QUESTIONS YOU SHOULD ASK BEFORE AUTOMATING.

Good automation starts with clear expectations. Here are the questions we expect serious clients to ask.

What does Flow Vello actually automate?

We automate repetitive business workflows, customer conversations, lead handling, support tasks, data movement and reporting processes.

Can you work with our existing tools?

Yes. We design integrations around the tools already used by the business where practical, instead of replacing everything unnecessarily.

How long does an automation project take?

Simple workflow projects can be delivered quickly; multi-system AI agents and dashboards require more discovery, testing and integration work. Exact timing is scoped before build.

Will AI replace our support or sales team?

No blanket promise. We automate the repetitive layer and keep humans in the loop for exceptions, sensitive cases and decisions that require judgment.

Do you provide post-launch support?

Yes. Support and optimization can continue after launch so the system remains reliable as workflows, tools and business requirements change.
