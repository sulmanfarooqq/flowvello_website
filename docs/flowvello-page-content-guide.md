## privacy-policy.html

Do not copy Proto's text — their policy describes their own data handling, which is legally specific to them, not you. Minimum sections a real privacy policy needs:
1. What data you collect (contact form fields, analytics/cookies if any)
2. Why you collect it (respond to inquiries, send proposals)
3. Whether you share it with third parties (e.g. email provider, CRM)
4. How long you retain it
5. How someone can request deletion
6. Contact email for privacy requests

If you don't have a lawyer, at minimum have this reviewed before publishing — a wrong or copied privacy policy is a bigger liability than not having a fancy homepage animation.
