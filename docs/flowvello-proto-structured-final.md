| Finance & Accounting services | ❌ Do not include — completely unrelated to your capability, and finance mistakes carry real liability |

**My recommendation:** Build the site with 6 pillars, not Proto's 4 — your existing 6 automation/dashboard services, reframed as "AAA" (Automation, AI Agents, and Analytics/dashboards) plus **Custom Software Development** if you can genuinely staff it. Skip BPO and Finance entirely; they're not your business and bolting them on for the sake of looking like Proto will get you into commitments you can't keep.

Everything below assumes: **6 core services + optional Custom Software Development = 7 pillars total.** Delete the software dev pillar throughout if you decide not to offer it.
